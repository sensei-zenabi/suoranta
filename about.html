About

BUDOSTACK is a software project by Ville Suoranta, developing a collection of legal and entertaining tools and games for retro computing. All applications are designed to run on original MS-DOS hardware or through modern emulators.

As a professional nerd, computers have been close to my heart since 1990. Every now and then, it feels good to escape the modern world and return to a simpler, internet-free environment where distractions are few and you can focus on your own thoughts rather than the endless stream of content fed to you by algorithms.

With that in mind, I wish you happy retro computing and hope you find as much joy in these applications as I do.

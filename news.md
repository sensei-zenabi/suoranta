## BUDOSTACK for MS-DOS

BUDOSTACK now focuses on standalone MS-DOS software for 486 PCs and DOSBox. The new application catalog has individual ZIP downloads and screenshots taken while running the programs.

Start with [WRITE](./#app-write) to draft a manuscript, [FLOW](./#app-flow) to diagram a process, or [EXPLORER](./#app-explorer) to manage files in two panes.

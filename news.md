## BUDOSTACK for MS-DOS

In the age of AI finding time to actually utilize your brains can be
difficult. The pace of engineering and science is accelerating, and 
often in worklife one cannot anymore afford spending time to search, 
learn and memorize information like we used to do it. This can cause
our brains to evolve into direction from which science does not yet
have enough information.

BUDOSTACK is a collection of applications designed for MS-DOS with one
goal in mind: force the user to process and produce information without
the help of AI. Easiest way to get started is to download DOSBOX into
your preferred device, and install the BUDOSTACK application suite.

For maximum focus, we recommend setting up an environment without 
internet connection. An old desktop computer will work nicely for this
purpose. If possible, try using old 5:4 or 4:3 display - you might be
positively surprised!

Start with [WRITE](./#app-write) to draft a manuscript, [FLOW](./#app-flow) to diagram a process, or [EXPLORER](./#app-explorer) to manage files in two panes.
